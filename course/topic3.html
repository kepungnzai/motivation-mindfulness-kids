🎨 Self-Determination

🎨

Self-Determination is a big word that means you are the boss of your own learning! Scientists Richard Ryan and Edward Deci found 3 secret ingredients that make you feel motivated and happy! 🗝️✨

🗝️ 🎮 🤝 📈 ⭐

🗝️ The 3 Secret Ingredients

Self-Determination Theory says everyone needs these 3 things to feel motivated:

  • 🎮 Autonomy (say: aw-TON-uh-mee) - Being in control! When you get to make choices about what and how you learn, you feel more excited! Like choosing which book to read or which game to play!
  • 📈 Competence (say: COM-pih-tenss) - Getting better! When you practice and improve at something, it feels amazing! Like leveling up in a video game!
  • 🤝 Relatedness (say: ree-LAY-ted-ness) - Connecting with others! When you learn and play with friends and family, it's more fun! We all need to feel like we belong! 💕

📖 The Story of the Two Artists

Once there were two artists. One was told exactly what to paint, what colors to use, and how to do it. The other got to choose their own subject, colors, and style. Which artist do you think was more excited to paint? The second one, of course! When we have autonomy (choice), our motivation soars! 🎨✨

🎮 Activity: My Choice Chart

Create a "Choice Chart" for your day. Write down things you can choose:

  • 📚 Choose your reading: Which book looks interesting to you?
  • 🎨 Choose your creative time: Draw, build, or write?
  • 🏃 Choose your movement: Dance, run, or stretch?
  • 🧩 Choose your challenge: Puzzle, game, or problem to solve?

Making choices makes your motivation grow! 🌱

💡 The "Goldilocks" Challenge Rule

For motivation to stay strong, challenges need to be "just right" - not too easy (boring!) and not too hard (frustrating!). Like Goldilocks finding the perfect porridge! When a challenge is just right, you enter a state called "flow" - where you're so focused and having so much fun that time seems to fly by! ⏰✨
