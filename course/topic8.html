🕵️ CBT - The Thought Detective

🕵️

CBT stands for Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (say: COG-nih-tiv Bee-HAYV-yer THAIR-uh-pee). It's a superpower that helps you understand how your thoughts, feelings, and actions are all connected! Like a detective, you learn to investigate your thoughts and find clues! 🔍✨

🕵️ 💭 ❤️ 🏃 🔍

🔗 The CBT Triangle: Thoughts → Feelings → Actions

Dr. Aaron Beck and Dr. Albert Ellis discovered that these three things are connected like a triangle:

💭 Thoughts ⬇️⬆️ ❤️ Feelings ⬇️⬆️ 🏃 Actions
  • 💭 Thoughts are what you think about a situation
  • ❤️ Feelings are how you feel because of those thoughts
  • 🏃 Actions are what you do because of those feelings

The amazing news? If you change your thoughts, you can change your feelings and actions too! That's the superpower of CBT! 🦸

📖 The Story of Sam and the Spelling Test

Let's follow Sam through two different ways of thinking about the same situation!

📝 The Situation: Sam's teacher announces a spelling test tomorrow

🔴 Path A: The Unhelpful Thought Trap

💭 Thought: "I'm terrible at spelling. I'm going to fail. Everyone will laugh at me!"
⬇️
❤️ Feeling: Scared 😨, worried 😟, sick to my stomach 🤢
⬇️
🏃 Action: Sam doesn't study, feels even worse, and gets a bad grade. "See? I told you I was terrible!" 😭

This is a Thought Trap! The thought made Sam feel bad and give up!

🟢 Path B: The Thought Detective (CBT Superpower!)

💭 CATCH the thought: "I notice I'm thinking 'I'm terrible at spelling.' Is that really true?"
⬇️
🔍 CHECK the evidence: "Well, I spelled 3 words right yesterday. I passed my last test. Maybe I'm not terrible - I just need to practice!"
⬇️
💭 CHANGE the thought: "Spelling is hard for me, but I can get better if I practice. I'll study 10 minutes tonight."
⬇️
❤️ NEW Feeling: Hopeful 🌟, a little nervous but okay 💪, ready to try
⬇️
🏃 NEW Action: Sam studies for 10 minutes, feels proud for trying, and does better on the test! 🎉

Sam used the CBT Superpower to catch, check, and change unhelpful thoughts! 🦸

🕵️ The Thought Detective's 3-Step Process

Here's the simple 3-step CBT process you can use anytime:

🎣

Step 1: CATCH

Notice the thought. What are you telling yourself? "I notice I'm thinking..."

🔍

Step 2: CHECK

Look for evidence. Is this thought 100% true? "What's another way to see this?"

🔄

Step 3: CHANGE

Swap the unhelpful thought for a helpful one. "A more helpful thought is..."

💡 Top 10 Thought Traps (Cognitive Distortions)

Here are the 10 most common thought traps our brains fall into. For each one, see the 🔴 unhelpful thought, the 🟢 helpful replacement, and a 😂 sarcastic roast to remind you how silly the trap sounds!

# Trap Name What It Means 🔴 Unhelpful → 🟢 Helpful 😂 Sarcastic Roast
1 🔮 Fortune Telling Predicting the future will be bad without evidence "I KNOW I'll mess up the presentation!"
→ "I don't know what will happen. I'll prepare and do my best." 		Oh wow, you can see the future now? Can you also tell me next week's lottery numbers? Since you're SO sure you'll fail, at least make some money off it! 🔮🙄
2 🔬 Mind Reading Assuming you know what others are thinking "My friend thinks I'm boring."
→ "I don't actually know what they think. I could ask them!" 		Oh, you're a mind reader now? Quick, what number am I thinking of? Wrong! It was 42. You're terrible at this. Maybe stick to asking people what they actually think? 🧠🙃
3 ⚫ All-or-Nothing Seeing things as only perfect or total failure "If I don't get 100%, I'm a failure."
→ "I can be proud of any progress. Every step forward counts!" 		Right, so if you eat 1 chip, the whole bag is ruined? Might as well eat the rest then. Congratulations, you just invented the "I finished the whole bag" diet! 🥨👏
4 🔍 Magnifying Making small problems seem huge "I made one mistake, so the whole day is ruined!"
→ "One mistake doesn't ruin everything. I can fix it and move on." 		You dropped a pea and now the whole kitchen is a disaster zone? Should we call the fire department? Or maybe... just pick it up? 🚨🫛😅
5 🏷️ Labeling Giving yourself or others a negative label "I forgot my homework. I'm so stupid."
→ "I forgot something today. That doesn't make me stupid - I can do better tomorrow." 		You dropped a pencil and now you're "Clumsy Clown-Fingers" for life? Welcome to the circus! We have a lifetime membership with your name on it! 🤡✏️🎪
6 🎭 Emotional Reasoning Believing your feelings are facts "I feel scared, so something bad MUST be about to happen."
→ "I feel scared, but feelings aren't facts. I can feel scared AND be safe." 		Oh, you feel like a unicorn? Then you MUST be one! Please show me your magical powers. Oh wait, you're just eating hay? That's just... being a horse with a party hat. 🦄😐
7 🧂 Should Statements Putting pressure on yourself with "should" "I SHOULD be perfect. I SHOULD never make mistakes."
→ "I'd LIKE to do well, but it's okay to make mistakes. I'm human!" 		You SHOULD be able to fly. You SHOULD breathe underwater. You SHOULD have been born a dolphin. But you're not, so maybe stop "should-ing" all over yourself? 🐬🤷
8 🔭 Mental Filter Only seeing the bad, ignoring the good "I got 9 out of 10 right, but I ONLY focus on the one I missed."
→ "I got 9 right! That's great! I'll practice the one I missed." 		You found 100 chocolate bars but ONE has a tiny dent. Obviously the whole batch is ruined. Let's just throw away the 99 perfect ones. Brilliant logic! 🍫👏
9 🌪️ Catastrophizing Imagining the worst possible outcome "If I trip during my school play, everyone will laugh FOREVER!"
→ "If I trip, people might chuckle for a second, then we all move on. It's not the end of the world." 		You sneezed in class. The roof is collapsing, aliens are invading, and you're being banished from Earth! Quick, while you're still here, can you grab me a snack? ACHOO! 😱
10 ⚖️ Personalization Blaming yourself for things outside your control "My friend is quiet today. It MUST be because I did something wrong."
→ "My friend might be tired or having a tough day. It's probably not about me." 		It rained today and you think it's because you forgot to water the plants? Of course! You have weather-controlling powers! Next time you're mad, just don't water the plants and blame the hurricane on yourself. ☔😅🌪️

🧠 Remember: Thoughts are NOT facts! Just because you think something doesn't make it true. Be a Thought Detective and CATCH → CHECK → CHANGE! 🕵️✨

🎮 Activity: Be a Thought Detective!

Think of a time you felt upset, worried, or angry. Now investigate!

  • 📝 The Situation: What happened?
  • 💭 CATCH the thought: What went through your mind?
  • ❤️ How did you feel? What did your body feel like?
  • 🏃 What did you do? How did you act?
  • 🔍 CHECK the evidence: Is the thought 100% true? What's another way to see it?
  • 🔄 CHANGE the thought: What's a more helpful thought?

Try this the next time you catch yourself in a thought trap! 🕵️✨

🕵️

🌟 Key Takeaways

  • 🔗 Thoughts → Feelings → Actions are all connected (CBT Triangle)
  • 🎣 CATCH unhelpful thoughts when they appear
  • 🔍 CHECK the evidence - is the thought really true?
  • 🔄 CHANGE to a more helpful, balanced thought
  • 🧠 Thoughts are not facts! You can question them
  • 🕵️ Be a Thought Detective - investigate your thinking!
